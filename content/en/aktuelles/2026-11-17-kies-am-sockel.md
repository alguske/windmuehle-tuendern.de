After exposing the base in October, the work went on. Many helping hands protected the base of the mill with gravel and tidied up the grounds.

## Gravel for the base

The gravel arrived on a trailer. We unloaded it shovel by shovel and spread it all around the mill tower. Then we raked it smooth.

Now rainwater can drain away, and the base stays dry.

## A new flower bed at the mill

Next to the gravel strip there is now a new flower bed. Bark mulch covers the soil, and rhododendrons grow there now. The area around the mill looks much tidier.

After the work there was coffee and cake inside the mill. Thank you to everyone who lent a hand.
